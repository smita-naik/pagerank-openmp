Instead of assigning approximately equal numbers of vertices to threads, use incoming-edge counts to determine workload distribution.

Requirements:

- Calculate prefix sums of per-vertex edge counts.
- Partition rows into contiguous ranges with approximately equal edge-processing workloads.
- Build partitions efficiently.
- Reuse the partitioning across PageRank iterations.
- Support arbitrary thread counts.
- Handle skewed graph distributions and high-degree vertices correctly.
- Avoid unnecessary synchronization.

Compare this strategy with the standard OpenMP scheduling approaches.

Explain its limitations, including cases where balancing edge counts alone cannot eliminate imbalance, and cases where partition construction or memory effects outweigh the benefits.
